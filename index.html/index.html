Peter Lundy | Environmental Mapping & GIS Portfolio

About Me

I am an environmental studies student developing skills in Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and spatial analysis. My work focuses on understanding how environmental systems, infrastructure, and human activity interact across space, with an emphasis on practical, real-world application.

What This Portfolio Shows

  • Interactive web maps built using Leaflet
  • Use of real-world spatial data (GeoJSON and external datasets)
  • Data visualization techniques such as proportional symbols and choropleths
  • User interaction through click events and dynamic feedback

Course Progression

  • Started with basic map setup, markers, and popups
  • Moved into symbol scaling and data-driven visualization
  • Developed interactive mapping tools and user-based inputs
  • Applied GIS concepts to environmental and spatial patterns
